# User Feedback – WE FOR EARTH

Feedback shared by 10 people who tried the platform.

1. **Amazing platform:** one place to learn about climate action and track progress.
2. **Easy to navigate:** the concept is clear and friendly for first-time users.
3. **Very relevant for youth:** useful for students who want to start with sustainability.
4. **Strong learning + tracking combination:** people liked having both in one place.
5. **Interest in mentoring:** several people showed interest in becoming mentors.
6. **High potential as an app:** many felt a mobile app would work even better.
7. **University collaboration potential:** universities and colleges can support it.
8. **Institutional partnerships:** schools can use it for awareness drives and student engagement.
9. **Scalable idea:** it can expand to more cities, campuses and communities.
10. **Timely and needed:** everyone agreed a platform like this is needed now.

## What we will do with it
- Build a mobile app version
- Start a mentor network
- Pilot with a university and schools
- Add videos, blogs and images to the lessons
